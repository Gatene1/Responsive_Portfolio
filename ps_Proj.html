What is Pattern Scrutiny?

Pattern Scrutiny is a breakdown of gameplay design patterns — focusing on how they actually function in real gameplay, not just theory.

Each issue compares related patterns, highlights tradeoffs, and ends with practical takeaways you can apply directly to your own games.

Inside Issue #01

  • Anchor(Main) Pattern: Risk vs Reward
  • Similar Pattern: Push Your Luck
  • Contrasting Pattern: Guaranteed Progression
  • Comparative analysis across all three
  • Designer takeaway section

Why This Exists

Most design resources stop at definitions.

Pattern Scrutiny focuses on how patterns interact, where they break, and how to use them intentionally — especially when multiple patterns compete inside the same system.
