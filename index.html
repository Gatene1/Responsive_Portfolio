About David & OutBox Games

I’m an independent game designer and programmer behind OutBox Games – a small studio where I explore game design patterns, build my own tools, and use AI as a creative partner rather than a shortcut.

  • Glean mini-games – small experiments that embody core gameplay patterns.
  • Actionary tools – Topic Creator, PatternChooser, and friends for structured ideation.
  • SpriteGrid – a custom sprite editor for tile-based HTML5 projects.
  • Game jams – testing ideas fast with collaborators and constraints.
  • Future: PatternCodex & VR / BCI-friendly experiments.

The OutBox Games Ecosystem

OutBox Games is more than a collection of projects – it’s an ecosystem. I use Actionary for ideas, SpriteGrid and friends for production, and a growing library of games to put everything into practice.
