About David & OutBox Games

I’m an independent game designer and programmer building OutBox Games – a tiny studio focused on AI-assisted design, retro-inspired games, and tools that help other creators think in game design patterns.

  • Glean mini-games – small experiments that embody core gameplay patterns.
  • Actionary tools – Topic Creator, PatternChooser, and friends for structured ideation.
  • SpriteGrid – a custom sprite editor for tile-based HTML5 projects.
  • Game jams – testing ideas fast with collaborators and constraints.
  • Future: PatternCodex & VR / BCI-friendly experiments.
